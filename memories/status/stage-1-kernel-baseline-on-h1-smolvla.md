# Stage-1 kernel baseline on H1: SmolVLA

## Summary
Stage 1 fixed its hardware, workload and kernel set, then measured every kernel and composed a per-call time. Hardware H1: an 8 x 8 SoftHier mesh with RedMulE 32 x 32 per cluster. Workload W1: SmolVLA on the VLABench observation format (three cameras). Every operator of W1 was run on H1 as a hand-written kernel, alone, with its output checked. Summing those times gives 29.0 ms per policy call at 22.0 % MFU (estimate). The vision encoder takes half the time; the action expert runs at the lowest MFU (7.4 %). This replaces the 79.2 ms LIBERO baseline of the previous entry, which ran on a different, smaller arch and is no longer a reference.

The note gives the diagram, then the evidence table (hardware and workload, kernels, composed result, what was removed), then open questions.

## Open questions
- Attention is timed unmasked; the prefix and the expert need a mask.
- The GEMM tile search is not exhaustive (about 10 settings per shape); the owner chose a cost model over wider sweeps as the next step.
- FlatAttention in async mode with an 8 x 4 group gives wrong output; cause not found.
- The composed time is a sum of standalone runs: overlap between kernels, the layout change between projections and attention, and keeping data on chip between kernels are not modelled.
- HBM bandwidth per channel: DRAMSys's utilization report implies 64 GB/s, the memspec 32 GB/s.
