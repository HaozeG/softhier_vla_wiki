# Evaluation and models

## Summary
Benchmark names, device names, one-line identities of the models the notes compare (organisation and design family only; sizes and speeds are in the model notes and in `knowledge/`), and the evidence tags on source notes. The tag meanings are the wiki's own convention and are listed by name at the start of project sessions. The [catalog of model notes](../resources/models/_overview.md) lists every model source.

## Terms
| Term                                           | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Scope      | Defined in                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LIBERO, Meta-World                             | Simulated robot-manipulation benchmarks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | field      | [SmolVLA](../knowledge/smolvla.md)                                                                       |
| SO100, SO101                                   | Low-cost real robot arms used for real-world tests.                                                                                                                                                                                                             | field      | [SmolVLA](../knowledge/smolvla.md)                                                                       |
| success rate                                   | Share of benchmark episodes in which the task is completed.                                                                                                                                                                                                     | field      | [SmolVLA](../knowledge/smolvla.md)                                                                       |
| tag recent                                     | A source posted within a few months of ingest or not independently reproduced.                                                                                                                                                                                  | convention | [wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md) |
| tag low-evidence                               | A source with latency-only claims, unclear devices or no task success.                                                                                                                                                                                          | convention | [wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md) |
| tag first-party                                | A vendor or company page: marketing-level unless numbers are given.                                                                                                                                                                                             | convention | [wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md) |
| tag community                                  | A forum post or community repository.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | convention | [wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md) |
| RT-2                                           | Google DeepMind's vision-language-action model: a large vision-language model fine-tuned on web data and robot data, with actions written as text tokens. The paper that named the VLA.                                                                         | field      | [rt-2](../resources/models/rt-2.md)                                                                      |
| OpenVLA                                        | An open VLA from Stanford, Berkeley and partners, built on the Prismatic vision-language model and trained to write discretized action tokens.                                                                                                                  | field      | [openvla](../resources/models/openvla.md)                                                                |
| Octo                                           | An open generalist robot policy from Berkeley and partners: a small transformer with a diffusion action head, not built on a vision-language model.                                                                                                             | field      | [octo](../resources/models/octo.md)                                                                      |
| π0 (pi0)                                       | Physical Intelligence's flow-matching VLA: a vision-language model with a separate action expert that generates continuous action chunks.                                                                                                                       | field      | [pi0](../resources/models/pi0.md)                                                                        |
| π0.5 (pi0.5)                                   | The successor of π0 from Physical Intelligence: co-trained on heterogeneous data, it first writes a text subtask and then low-level actions, and adds a flow-matching action expert after pretraining with discrete FAST tokens.                                | field      | [pi05](../resources/models/pi05.md)                                                                      |
| GR00T N1                                       | NVIDIA's open dual-system VLA for humanoid robots: a vision-language model (system two) plus a diffusion transformer trained with flow matching (system one).                                                                                                   | field      | [gr00t-n1](../resources/models/gr00t-n1.md)                                                              |
| Helix                                          | Figure's VLA for humanoid upper-body control: a slow vision-language model plus a fast visuomotor policy, running on the robot's embedded GPUs; described only on a company blog page.                                                                          | field      | [figure-helix](../resources/models/figure-helix.md)                                                      |
| SmolVLA                                        | Hugging Face's small open VLA: a truncated SmolVLM backbone with a flow-matching action expert, plus an asynchronous inference stack.                                                                                                                           | field      | [smolvla](../resources/models/smolvla.md)                                                                |
| TinyVLA                                        | A small, fast VLA from ECNU and partners: a small Pythia-based vision-language model fine-tuned with LoRA and a diffusion policy head, without large-scale robot pretraining.                                                                                   | field      | [tinyvla](../resources/models/tinyvla.md)                                                                |
| OpenVLA-OFT                                    | A fine-tuning recipe for OpenVLA from Stanford: parallel decoding, action chunking, continuous actions and an L1 loss.                                                                                                                                          | field      | [openvla-oft](../resources/models/openvla-oft.md)                                                        |
| Gemini Robotics On-Device                      | Google DeepMind's VLA announced for running locally on robot devices without a data network; the announcement gives almost no technical detail.                                                                                                                 | field      | [gemini-robotics-on-device](../resources/models/gemini-robotics-on-device.md)                            |
| CALVIN                                         | A robot-manipulation benchmark; the compression notes report its score as an average length.                                                                                                                                                                    | field      | [dysl-vla](../resources/compression/dysl-vla.md)                                                         |
| SIMPLER                                        | A robot-manipulation benchmark on which the compression notes report average success.                                                                                                                                                                           | field      | [efficientvla](../resources/compression/efficientvla.md)                                                 |
| ALOHA                                          | A two-arm (bimanual) robot setup used in the sources, often controlled at a high rate.                                                                                                                                                                          | field      | [openvla-oft](../resources/models/openvla-oft.md)                                                        |
| RTX 4090, A100, H100                           | NVIDIA GPUs that the sources use as reference points, far above the small edge devices this project targets.                                                                                                                                                    | field      | [edge hardware](../knowledge/edge-hardware-and-the-10-tops-gap.md)                                       |
| pretraining, fine-tuning, frozen, from scratch | Pretraining: first training on large, broad data. Fine-tuning: further training on a smaller, task-specific set. Frozen: a part whose weights are not updated during training. From scratch: training without starting from a pretrained model. Standard gloss. | field      | [SmolVLA](../knowledge/smolvla.md)                                                                       |
| embodiment, cross-embodiment                   | Embodiment: the particular robot body, with its arms, sensors and action space. Cross-embodiment: using one model or dataset across several different robots.                                                                                                   | field      | [SmolVLA](../knowledge/smolvla.md)                                                                       |
| episode                                        | One attempt at a task, from the start until it succeeds or stops; benchmark scores such as the success rate are counted over episodes.                                                                                                                          | field      | [SmolVLA](../knowledge/smolvla.md)                                                                       |
| multi-task                                     | One model trained on several tasks at once instead of one model per task.                                                                                                                                                                                       | field      | [SmolVLA](../knowledge/smolvla.md)                                                                       |
| mAP                                            | Mean average precision: a score for object detectors, higher is better. The notes mention it when a quantized detector loses accuracy. Standard gloss.                                                                                                          | field      | [RK3588 deployment](../knowledge/rk3588-vla-deployment.md)                                               |
| SigLIP, DINOv2, PaliGemma, Prismatic           | SigLIP and DINOv2 are image encoders. PaliGemma is a vision-language model that π0 is built on. Prismatic is the vision-language model that OpenVLA is built on; it fuses the SigLIP and DINOv2 encoders with a Llama language model.                           | field      | [VLA architecture overview](../knowledge/vla-architecture-overview.md)                                   |
