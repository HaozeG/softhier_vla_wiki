# Evidence

## Summary
How the wiki marks the strength of a source and of a number: the evidence tags on source notes and the words estimate, measured, self-reported and inference. All rows are the wiki's own convention ([wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md); inference from [0013](../wiki-design/decisions/0013-scope-and-no-derived-content.md) and the [status and patch logs](../wiki-design/decisions/2026-11-12-status-and-patch-logs.md), whose entries label every claim measured, estimate or inference) and are listed by name at the start of project sessions.

## Terms
| Term                              | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Scope      | Defined in                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| estimate, measured, self-reported | Estimate: a figure computed from specs, configs or partial runs rather than run as a whole, by a source or, in the status and patch logs, by the project. Measured: a reported run. Self-reported: a community claim that was not independently checked. | convention | [wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md) |
| inference                         | Our own reasoning or arithmetic on sourced facts, which no source states and no run has checked; written only when the user asks or in the status and patch logs, and always labelled.                                                                   | convention | [status and patch logs](../wiki-design/decisions/2026-11-12-status-and-patch-logs.md)                    |
| tag recent                        | A source posted within a few months of ingest or not independently reproduced.                                                                                                                                                                           | convention | [wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md) |
| tag low-evidence                  | A source with latency-only claims, unclear devices or no task success.                                                                                                                                                                                   | convention | [wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md) |
| tag first-party                   | A vendor or company page: marketing-level unless numbers are given.                                                                                                                                                                                      | convention | [wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md) |
| tag community                     | A forum post or community repository.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | convention | [wiki-design decision 0004](../wiki-design/decisions/0004-evidence-tiers-for-fast-moving-vla-sources.md) |
