# Learning paradigms

## Summary
How a policy is trained: imitation of demonstrations, reinforcement learning from reward, and the training stages. All terms are standard (scope field). See [imitation learning and behaviour cloning](../knowledge/imitation-learning-and-behaviour-cloning.md) and [fast policy trained by RL](../knowledge/fast-policy-trained-by-rl.md).

## Terms
| Term                                            | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Scope | Defined in                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| imitation learning (IL), behaviour cloning (BC) | Training a policy to copy expert demonstrations. Behaviour cloning, its simplest form, treats it as supervised learning from observations to actions, with no reward.                                                                                           | field | [imitation learning and behaviour cloning](../knowledge/imitation-learning-and-behaviour-cloning.md) |
| compounding error, covariate shift              | In imitation, small errors move the robot into states the demonstrations never show, where the policy errs more; the mismatch of states is the covariate shift.                                                                                                 | field | [imitation learning and behaviour cloning](../knowledge/imitation-learning-and-behaviour-cloning.md) |
| multimodal demonstrations                       | Demonstrations that solve the same situation in several different, equally good ways. A regression policy averages them; a generative head can pick one.                                                                                                        | field | [imitation learning and behaviour cloning](../knowledge/imitation-learning-and-behaviour-cloning.md) |
| reinforcement learning (RL)                     | Training a policy from reward: the robot tries actions, receives a success or failure signal, and the policy is changed to make rewarded behaviour more likely. It contrasts with imitation learning, which copies demonstrations.                              | field | [fast policy trained by RL](../knowledge/fast-policy-trained-by-rl.md)                               |
| critic (value function)                         | A network that estimates how good a state or an action is, in terms of expected future reward. RL uses it to improve the policy, and some methods use it to pick among candidate actions.                                                                       | field | [fast policy trained by RL](../knowledge/fast-policy-trained-by-rl.md)                               |
| residual (edit) policy                          | A small policy that adds a bounded correction to the action proposed by a larger base policy, instead of producing the action from scratch.                                                                                                                     | field | [fast policy trained by RL](../knowledge/fast-policy-trained-by-rl.md)                               |
| advantage conditioning                          | Training a policy with an extra input that says whether an action was better or worse than expected, and then asking for the better case at run time. It lets RL update a flow-matching model without policy gradients.                                         | field | [fast policy trained by RL](../knowledge/fast-policy-trained-by-rl.md)                               |
| pretraining, fine-tuning, frozen, from scratch  | Pretraining: first training on large, broad data. Fine-tuning: further training on a smaller, task-specific set. Frozen: a part whose weights are not updated during training. From scratch: training without starting from a pretrained model. Standard gloss. | field | [SmolVLA](../knowledge/smolvla.md)                                                                   |
